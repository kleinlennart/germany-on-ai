# Three data layers, with judgments kept out of the Explorer

Records sit in three layers: Source records mirror official and published material as faithfully as possible, Annotations are machine-generated and name their method, and Judgments are an analyst's conclusions citing Source records. The Explorer shows only the first two, and Judgments appear only in the Report. Keeping interpretation apart from the record lets the Explorer serve as a neutral reference, and lets each conclusion be traced back to evidence anyone can inspect.
