# Germany on the Record: AI

A compiled, sourced record of how Germany engages on AI at home and internationally, and the analysis built on it.

## Layers

**Source record** (layer 1):
A record that mirrors one item from a Source as faithfully as possible: a printed paper, a speech, a vote, a lobby register entry, an official statement.
_Avoid_: raw data, entry, item

**Annotation** (layer 2):
A machine-generated label or text attached to a Source record, such as AI relevance, topics or an English summary, always naming the method that produced it.
_Avoid_: tag (when meaning the whole layer), enrichment, metadata

**Judgment** (layer 3):
An analyst's conclusion that cites one or more Source records, such as a position, a contradiction or a key event.
_Avoid_: finding, insight, assessment

## Sources and provenance

**Source**:
A publisher or dataset the project draws on or points to, such as DIP, the Lobbyregister or the UN Digital Library, described in the Catalogue.
_Avoid_: provider, feed, origin

**Catalogue**:
The registry of all known Sources, including ones the project only references, with their coverage, access, licence and status.
_Avoid_: source list, inventory

**Confidence**:
How well a record is supported by its source: high, medium or low. Required wherever a record is reconstructed rather than mirrored, such as a Trip.
_Avoid_: certainty, reliability score

**Publishability**:
Whether a Source's raw material and personal fields may appear in the public dataset, set per Source in the Catalogue.
_Avoid_: visibility, privacy flag

## Record types

**Actor**:
A person, ministry, parliamentary body, state, organisation or international organisation that acts on AI policy.
_Avoid_: stakeholder (reserve for the stakeholder view), entity

**Event**:
Something with a date and place at which Actors take part: a summit, a UN session, a Council meeting, a debate, a hearing, a press conference.
_Avoid_: meeting, occasion

**Trip**:
An Event reconstructed from public records in which a federal government member travels in an official capacity; always carries a Confidence.
_Avoid_: visit, journey

**Statement**:
Something an Actor said or published on the record, kept as a short excerpt with a link to the full text.
_Avoid_: quote, speech (when meaning the general type)

**Vote**:
One Actor's recorded choice on one item in a formal forum: yes, no, abstain or absent.
_Avoid_: ballot, position

**Document**:
A formal text such as a strategy, declaration, printed paper (Drucksache), report or law.
_Avoid_: file, paper (alone)

**Lobby entry**:
An organisation's registration in the Lobbyregister and the regulatory projects it names.
_Avoid_: lobby record, registration

**Topic**:
A term from the project's controlled vocabulary that a record can be about.
_Avoid_: category, theme, tag

## Products

**Explorer**:
The public website for navigating Source records and Annotations.
_Avoid_: tool, dashboard, app

**Report**:
The versioned synthesis that presents Judgments and cites the Explorer's records.
_Avoid_: paper, study
